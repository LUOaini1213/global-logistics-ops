Global logistics operations analytics

Personal portfolio project, October 2026, built with AI assistance. Five models for a network-equipment supply chain: warehouse slotting, inventory policy, landed cost by Incoterms 2020, a returns network, and overseas rollout logistics (transport options, ship dates, container loading, transport cost). The warehouse and demand data of Parts 1, 2 and 4 are synthetic and all rates are illustrative; Parts 1b and 2b re-run the slotting and inventory analyses on a real public order dataset (UCI Online Retail II, CC BY 4.0); Part 5 uses public trade shares, World Bank port/customs dwell times, airport coordinates and computed sea distances; a public port-throughput series is used as a seasonality proxy. Not tax, customs or legal advice.

1. Warehouse slotting

Single-block warehouse, 1,000 SKUs in 1,200 slots (10 aisles x 20 bays x 2 sides x 3 levels), depot front-left. Velocity and co-pick affinity are estimated on a training order history; every method is then scored on 3,000 held-out orders per seed by simulating S-shape and return routing. The affinity weight lambda = 0.5 was tuned on three development seeds that are not among the 20 evaluation seeds. Heavy (>15 kg) and large items are forced to floor level; medium items to the lower two levels.

Validation against an exact formula

For i.i.d. normal daily demand and a fixed lead time L = 10 d, a base-stock level S gives exact fill rate 1 - (E[(DL+1-S)+] - E[(DL-S)+])/mu, on-hand S - L mu + E[(DL-S)+] and no-stockout probability Phi(z). 20 replications x 20,000 days: 20/20 comparisons within 3 standard errors, 19/20 inside the 95% CI; largest fill-rate gap 0.03 pp. Inventory balance error < 2e-10 units.

Seasonality stress test (public data)

Monthly demand scaled by the Singapore port-throughput seasonal index (a proxy: port boxes are not SKU demand). The ocean (r,Q) policy tuned for flat demand delivers 95.0% on flat demand and 95.0% on seasonal demand; the weakest month is month 12 at 93.0%. The index ranges from -9% to +3% around the mean month, so the effect is small.

3. Landed cost by Incoterms 2020

Rates are ILLUSTRATIVE round numbers, not quotes. Singapore GST 9% and its valuation basis were checked against IRAS / Singapore Customs pages (2026-10-13); the EU-style and US-style duty and VAT rates are placeholders. The responsibility split is a model of the rules checked against ICC, trade.gov and two secondary summaries; the binding text is the ICC publication. Not tax advice.

S = seller pays (and re-invoices inside the price with a 5% markup), B = buyer pays. Insurance is an obligation only under CIF (ICC C) and CIP (ICC A); elsewhere the model charges it to the risk bearer.
